01WHAT IS JEV AI?

A model that
answers your code.

Jev is TypeSafe AI’s first public System One model: built for fast, bounded judgments inside software—not open-ended conversation.

Read TypeSafe’s official introduction ↗

Give it a state—a ticket, an agent trace, an invoice—and ask a typed question. Instead of a free-form answer to parse, Jev returns a value shaped for the question: a choice, a score on your rubric, or the probability that a statement is true.

That doesn’t make every decision correct, or make Jev a replacement for a general-purpose LLM. It changes the interface: from “generate something a person can read” to “return a signal my program can branch on.”

iThe important distinction: Jev is TypeSafe’s hosted model. DecisionOps is an independent, open-source evaluation workbench—not the model, its weights, or an official TypeSafe product.

03WHEN A JUDGMENT BECOMES A BRANCH

Every “if” has
a cost.

Hard-coded rules are brittle. A chat model may be more machinery than a narrow judgment needs. Jev points at a new middle ground: machine-readable judgment, composed with the rules you own.

01 / CUSTOMER OPS

Route the request.
Escalate the edge.

Classify intent, score urgency, and send low-confidence cases to a human—not to a guessed queue.

↗
02 / AGENT SYSTEMS

Review the run.
Not every run.

Score a completed agent trace for policy risk or silent failure, then apply your own review threshold.

↗
03 / DATA PIPELINES

Read the stream.
At machine scale.

Turn unstructured records into typed signals you can filter, rank, aggregate, and route downstream.

↗
04 / PRODUCT UX

Make it feel instant.
Keep a human fallback.

Use a narrow decision in a latency-sensitive path, with a threshold that matches the consequence.

↗

For high-stakes, ambiguous, or irreversible actions, typed output is not a substitute for domain rules, validation, or human review.
